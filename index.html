Install Assay to your home screen.It then opens full screen, works without a signal, and its storage stops being cleared automatically.

Settings

A date lets Assay show what buying Bitcoin instead would have done. The rate is used to work out what the balance grew from, so the comparison is fair to the cash.

What Assay counts in. Gold, silver, sats and months are all worked out from this, and it is the default for new holdings.

What you spend in a typical month. Assay divides what you hold by it, so the total can read 7.4 months instead of a cash figure. It stays on this device like everything else, and is used for nothing else.

Force update throws away the cached copy of the app and re-installs the service worker, which fixes a version that has got stuck. Your holdings are kept somewhere else entirely and are not touched by it.

Where it lives. Everything you enter stays in this browser's storage on this device. There is no account and no server holding your figures. Clearing site data, or deleting the app, deletes them — so take a backup.

What leaves the device. Only watched addresses. They're looked up on mempool.space, which means that server sees which addresses you asked about. Prices come from CoinGecko. Typed amounts are never sent anywhere.

Monero can't be watched by address. Stealth addresses mean an XMR address reveals nothing publicly — reading a balance needs your private view key, which this app will never ask for. Enter Monero amounts by hand.

Never enter a seed phrase or private key here. This app can only read public data. It cannot sign or send anything.
